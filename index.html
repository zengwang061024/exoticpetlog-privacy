Privacy Policy for ExoticPetLog

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data Collection

ExoticPetLog is an offline-first app. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All pet records and photos are stored locally on your iOS device.

2. Internet Connection

The app does not require an internet connection. We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Photo Library Access

We request photo library access solely to attach pictures to your pet's records. Photos are never uploaded.
